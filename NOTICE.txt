2. Optional third-party services
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The six proxied tools are inert until you supply credentials in
config/secrets.php. When configured, requests are forwarded server side to the
provider you choose, and your use of that provider is governed by its own terms
and privacy policy:

    CloudConvert v2 (default) - PDF to Word and Word to PDF, api/convert.php
    mail.tm (default)         - 10 minute disposable inbox, api/tempmail.php
    Any OpenAI-compatible API - text summarizer and code explainer, api/ai.php
    Public DNS over HTTPS resolvers - fallback path in api/dns.php

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the
providers above.
